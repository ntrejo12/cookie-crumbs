About
CookieCrumbs is an extension turned website that follows your cookie trail to see where your data goes and how it is being used. From finding 3rd party vendors to uncovering how many cookies are used, CookieCrumbs is your one stop cookie shop. Download it from Google Extension now and open up the cookie jar to learn more about the cookies that follow you around.
